EVALUATION METHODOLOGY

More than a single score.

Different targets and evaluation suites are never pooled into one ranking.

01

Estimation accuracy

Bias reports signed estimation error. Variance reports sampling variability, not standard error. RMSE summarizes squared estimation error. Each release specifies the target and aggregation.

02

Confidence intervals

Length is the mean interval width. Coverage is the fraction containing the true parameter, assessed against the nominal level. Shorter intervals alone do not imply better inference.

03

Distribution shifts & time

ID and OOD are evaluated separately. Each release identifies the split definition, hardware, and timing scope. Compare methods within the same evaluation setting.
